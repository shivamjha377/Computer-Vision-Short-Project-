Hi this is computer vision project in which i am tracking the yellow colour object in realtime using HSV(Hue saturation Value)
method.
In the project i have used the module, OpenCv, Numpy, Pillow
Opencv is for capturing the data from the camera 
Numpy is doing the mathematical compution like making of boundingbox, frame tracking
and Pillow for capturing the value of the required color range needed for the project.
